An agency will receive a monthly basic salary if it:

  • Meets the monthly Rubies target
  • Maintains the required number of active hosts
Definition: Active Host

A host is counted as active if they:

  • Complete at least 10 valid days in a month
  • Complete at least 20 valid hours in a month
  • Earn 100,000 Rubies or more in gifts in a month
⚠ Important

Failure to meet either the Monthly Revenue Target or Active Host Requirement will result in Host Activation Salary Policy disqualification.
