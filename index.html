💸 Withdrawal Guide

Salary Withdrawal

01
Host Agency Joining Process
📱 Steps

Step 1: Open App → Click Me Section → Click More → Click Host Center → Click Agency Section

Step 2: Enter your Agency ID, click Bind, then confirm the Agency you are joining.

🎉 Congratulations! You have joined your desired Agency.

02
Authentication & Bank Withdrawal (USA)
🇺🇸
USA Hosts Only

This option is available only for hosts based in the USA. If from any other country, use Section 3 (USDT) instead.

📱 Steps

Open App → Me Section → More → Host Center → Authentication

🪪 Identity Information
  • Name: Real name as per National ID Card
  • ID Number: National ID Number
  • WhatsApp: Number with Country Code
  • Gmail: Your Gmail address
📸 Photo Uploads
  • Photo ID Card: Upload front of your National ID
  • Photo Holding ID: Selfie holding your ID near face
  • Live Room Poster: Upload a good profile picture
🏦 Bank Payment Details
  • Payment Method: Choose "Bank_Transfer"
  • Bank Code: Enter your Bank Routing Number
  • Account Type: "Checking" or "Savings"
  • Account Name: Your bank account name
  • Account Number: Your bank account number
  • Address / State / City / Zipcode
✅ After Submission

Once filled, send your ID for approval. After approval you can start Streaming and withdrawing earnings.

⚠ Important Warning

Ensure all bank details are entered exactly as registered — incorrect details may cause failed or delayed payments.

Open App → Me Section → More → Host Center → Authentication

  • Name: Real name as per National ID Card
  • ID Number: National ID Number
  • WhatsApp: Number with Country Code
  • Gmail: Gmail address
  • Photo ID Card: Front of National ID
  • Photo Holding ID: Selfie with ID near face
  • Live Room Poster: Good profile picture
  • Country/Region: Select from dropdown
💰 USDT Payment Details
  • Payment Method: Choose "C-Wallet" for USDT
  • Currency: USDT — automatically selected
  • Blockchain Network: Select from: TronERCSOLBSC
  • Wallet Address: Enter address matching chosen network
✅ After Submission

Once filled, send your ID for approval. After approval you can start Streaming and withdrawing earnings.

⚠ Important Warning

Ensure wallet address matches chosen blockchain exactly — incorrect address may result in permanent loss of funds.

04
How to Apply for Withdrawal
📱 Steps

Open App → Me Section → My Ruby → WD (Withdraw) → Enter value in Rubies

🔥 Example

To withdraw $100 USD, enter 2,000,000 Rubies in the withdrawal field.

  • Rate: 20,000 Ruby = 1 USD
  • Frequency: Once per day
  • Minimum: $10 USD
