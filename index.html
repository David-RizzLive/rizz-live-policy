💸 Withdrawal Guide
Salary Withdrawal
1
Host Agency Joining Process
⌄
📱 Steps

Step 1: Open App → Click Me Section → Click More → Click Host Center → Click Agency Section

Step 2: Enter your Agency ID, click Bind, then confirm the Agency you are joining.

🎉 Congratulations! You have joined your desired Agency.

2
Authentication & Bank Withdrawal (USA only)
⌄
🇺🇸
USA Hosts Only

This option is available only for hosts based in the USA. If you are from any other country, please use Section 3 (USDT Withdrawal) instead.

📱 Steps

Open App → Me Section → More → Host Center → Authentication

🪪 Identity Information
  • Name: Enter your real name as per National ID Card
  • ID Number: Enter your National ID Number
  • WhatsApp: Enter your number with Country Code
  • Gmail: Enter your Gmail address
📸 Photo Uploads
  • Photo ID Card: Upload front of your National ID
  • Photo Holding ID: Selfie holding your ID near your face
  • Live Room Poster: Upload a good profile picture
🌍 Region Info
  • Country/Region: Select your country
🏦 Bank Payment Details
  • Payment Method: Choose "Bank_Transfer"
  • Bank Code: Enter your Bank Routing Number
  • Account Type: "Checking" or "Savings"
  • Account Name: Your bank account name
  • Account Number: Your bank account number
  • Address / State / City / Zipcode: Bank address details
✅ After Submission

Once the Authentication Form is filled, send your ID for approval. After approval you can start Streaming and withdrawing your earnings.

⚠ Important Warning

Make sure all bank details are entered exactly as registered with your bank — incorrect details may cause failed or delayed payments.

  • Payment Method: Choose "C-Wallet" for USDT Withdrawal
  • Currency: USDT — automatically selected
  • Blockchain Network: Select your network from:
    Tron ERC SOL BSC
  • Wallet Address: Enter your wallet address for the same blockchain network you selected above

Once the Authentication Form is filled, send your ID for approval. After approval you can start Streaming and withdrawing your earnings.

⚠ Important Warning

Make sure the wallet address matches the chosen blockchain exactly — an incorrect address may result in permanent loss of funds.
